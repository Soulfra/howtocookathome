Why bother cooking at home?

Eating out adds up fast. A normal dinner is ~$25; the same dinner at home is ~$8. Swap a few nights a week and you're pocketing $50+ you didn't have before.

We track the tally in the weekly email. What you do with it — leave a bigger tip at a place you love, save for a show, or just stop flinching at the bank balance — is up to you.
