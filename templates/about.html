The Mission

How To Cook At Home started with a simple question: why is it so hard to find cooking instruction that actually teaches you to cook?

Most recipe sites assume you already know the basics. Most cooking shows prioritize entertainment over education. We do neither. Every episode answers a real question — the kind you'd actually type into a search engine — and builds your skills from the ground up.

Start at Episode 1 and work through all 30, or jump to whatever you need right now. Either way works. No subscriptions, no paywalls on the basics.

How We Teach

Clear, confident, no-nonsense instruction. Like having a friend who went to culinary school explain things at your kitchen counter. We tell you what to do, why it works, and what to watch for — then let you cook.

Every recipe includes USDA-sourced nutrition data, real ingredient costs, and technique breakdowns. No filler content, no personality-driven fluff, no gimmicks.

The Platform

HTCAH isn't just a recipe site — it's a multi-tenant platform that helps restaurants and food businesses get online with real data behind them.

USDA Nutrition

Every ingredient matched against the USDA FoodData Central database. Real numbers, not estimates.

Menu Engineering

Stars, plowhorses, puzzles, dogs — know which dishes make money and which ones don't.

Food Costing

Upload your vendor invoices. Get per-dish costs, suggested pricing, and margin analysis.

Branded Sites

Your restaurant gets its own page with your colors, your menu, your recipes — in minutes.

UPC Scanner

Scan product barcodes to pull ingredient data directly from USDA and OpenFoodFacts.

No Tracking

Token-based auth, no cookies, no ads, no third-party analytics. Your data stays yours.
